# Using BrewCRM: a guide for the sales team

BrewCRM is the brewery's shared notebook for selling beer. It remembers every bar, restaurant and hotel you talk to, what you said, what they want, and when to get back to them. It also shows how close each one is to ordering.

You don't need any technical knowledge. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use BrewCRM.

**The one habit that makes it work:** after every visit, call, tasting or message, take 30 seconds to **log it** and **set the next follow-up date**. Everything else in BrewCRM is built on that.

## 1. Getting in

1. Your manager creates your account and sends you the BrewCRM web address, your email and a **temporary password**.
2. Open the address on your phone or computer and sign in.
3. BrewCRM asks you to **choose your own password**. The checklist under the box turns green as you meet each rule: at least 12 characters, a letter and a number, a symbol (unless it's 16+ characters), and not your email name. Tap **Show** to see what you've typed.
4. You're in. On your phone, add it to your home screen so it opens like an app:
   - **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
   - **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → *Add to Home screen*.

**Forgot your password?** Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. If email is set up you'll get a reset link (it works for 30 minutes); otherwise the page tells you to ask your manager for a new temporary password.

## 2. The words BrewCRM uses

| Word | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Account** | A place you sell to, or want to | *Bar La Esquina, Escazú* |
| **Contact** | A person at that place | *María, owner*. The ★ marks the main contact |
| **Deal** | One opportunity with an account: what you're trying to sell them | *2 taps of IPA, ₡150,000/month* |
| **Stage** | How far a deal has got (below) | *Sampling* |
| **Activity** | Anything that happened: a visit, call, WhatsApp, email, tasting, event, order | *Tasted the IPA and the lager* |
| **Follow-up** | The date you'll get back to them | *In 2 weeks* |
| **Lead** | A place found on Google Maps that isn't an account yet | *Pub El Faro, Santa Ana* |

### The stages of a deal

Every deal moves left to right through these stages, like beer moving from the brewhouse to the glass:

| Stage | Means | You usually move it here when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Prospect** | On your list, no contact yet | You add the account |
| **Contacted** | You've talked to them | Your first visit or call. BrewCRM does this for you |
| **Sampling** | They've tasted your beer | You log a tasting. BrewCRM does this for you |
| **Proposal** | You've given prices or an offer | You send the price list or quote |
| **First order** | They've bought once | Automatic with Loyverse, or by hand |
| **Recurring** | They buy regularly | Automatic after the second order |
| **Dormant** | They used to buy, but haven't lately | Automatic after 45 days with no order |
| **Lost** | It didn't work out | You mark it and write why |

## 3. The screens

The green bar at the top has the main screens. On the right is **Log activity**, which works from anywhere.

### Pipeline

*The big picture: every deal, by stage.*

- Each column is a stage. Its header shows how many deals it holds, their total value per month, and their liters per month.
- Each **card** is a deal: the account name, the deal title and area, its value and liters, the follow-up date and the next step. Red means the follow-up is overdue.
- **To move a deal:** drag the card to another column. Or use the stage menu at the bottom of the card, which is easier on a phone. On a touchscreen, press and hold the card for a moment, then drag.
- **Filter by account or area** narrows the board, e.g. type `Heredia`.
- **Show lost** includes lost deals, which are hidden by default.
- **New deal** adds an opportunity to an existing account.
- Tap an account name to open its page.

The columns wrap onto more rows on smaller screens, so scroll down to see them all.

### Follow-ups

*Your to-do list. Open this first every morning.*

- **The top list:** everything **due now** (in red) and **scheduled** over the next 7 days, soonest first. Each line has **Call** (when the account has a phone number) and **Log activity**.
- **Going quiet:** accounts with **no activity in 3 weeks**. These are customers slipping away, so give them a call or visit.

If the list says *Nothing due this week*, you haven't been setting follow-up dates when you log activities.

### Accounts

*Every bar, restaurant, hotel and shop.*

- **Search** by name, area or address, and filter by **Type** (bar, restaurant, hotel, café, retail, supermarket, event, distributor, other).
- Each line shows the last activity and, for customers, their number of orders.
- **Log activity** right from the list, or tap the name to open the account.
- **New account** adds one by hand: name, type and area are enough to start. BrewCRM also creates a *Draft placement* deal in **Prospect** for it.

### An account's page

*Everything about one place on one screen.*

- **Top:** name, type, area and the next follow-up.
- **Details:** address, phone, website, notes.
  - The phone has a **WhatsApp** link that opens a chat directly.
  - Use **Notes** for things you should remember, like *"Owner prefers IPAs. Open Tue–Sun. Deliveries before 3 pm."*
  - **Edit** changes them.
- **Contacts:** the people.
  - **Add contact** with their name, role, email, phone and WhatsApp if it's different from their phone.
  - Tick **Main contact** for the decision maker (★).
  - If you add their email, Gmail messages with them appear here automatically (see [section 5](#5-what-brewcrm-does-on-its-own)).
- **Deals:** what you're selling them.
  - **Add deal** and fill in:
    - **Title**, e.g. *2 taps*.
    - **Stage**.
    - **Value per month** and **est. liters per month**. These add up in the Pipeline headers.
    - **Next step**, e.g. *Send price list*.
    - **Next follow-up**.
  - When a deal is lost, set the stage to **Lost** and fill in **Lost reason**, e.g. *Exclusive contract with a big brand*. Those reasons show you why you lose over time.
- **Loyverse:** **Link customer** connects the account to its customer in the brewery's point of sale, so its orders show up here (ask your manager if you can't find it).
- **Activity timeline:** everything that happened, newest first:
  - your visits, calls and tastings;
  - emails (marked *gmail*, with **Open in Gmail**);
  - orders from Loyverse, with what they bought.

  Each entry is marked *from us* or *from them*. Use the **Log activity** button at the top of the page to add one, and **Delete** to remove a mistake (emails and orders can't be deleted).

### Leads

*Finding new places to sell to, from Google Maps.*

1. In **What**, type a kind of place, or tap a ready-made one: *bar, restaurante, hotel, licorera, cervecería artesanal, pub, gastropub, minisúper*.
2. In **Where**, type an area: *Escazú*, *Heredia*, *Tamarindo*…
3. Tap **Search Google Maps**. You get 20 results; **Load 20 more** fetches the next page.
4. For each result:
   - **Map** opens it in Google Maps, so you can check the photos and reviews.
   - **Get phone** looks up its phone number and website.
   - **Add as account** turns it into an account with a *Draft placement* deal in **Prospect**. It's now on your Pipeline.
   - **Dismiss** if it's not a fit: closed, a chain, doesn't serve beer. It won't clutter the list again.
5. The menu at the top switches between **New**, **Converted** and **Dismissed** leads.

The counter (*"120 of 4500 searches used"*) is the brewery's free monthly allowance from Google, shared by the team. Searches and phone lookups are free up to that limit, so don't worry about using them, but don't run the same search ten times either.

### Settings

- **Your password:** change it any time (**Show** reveals what you type).
- **Integrations** show whether Gmail, Loyverse, Google Maps and password emails are connected. Red boxes mean something needs your manager's attention.
- **Sync now** fetches new emails or orders immediately instead of waiting.
- **Sign out.** Do this on shared computers.

The small **version label** next to "BrewCRM" opens build details. Use **Copy details** if you ever need to report a problem.

## 4. Logging an activity (the most important button)

Tap **Log activity** in the top bar, on an account page, in Follow-ups, or on the Accounts list:

1. **Account:** if you started from the top bar, pick the account; otherwise it's already chosen.
2. **Type:** Visit, Call, WhatsApp, Email, Tasting, Event, Order or Other.
3. **Direction:** *We reached out* or *They reached out*. Choose the second when they called you.
4. **Contact:** who you talked to (optional).
5. **What happened:** a sentence or two. Write it for a colleague who has never been there:
   - Good: *"Met María. Tasted IPA + lager, loved the IPA. Wants a price for 2 taps. Decides after the 15th."*
   - Not useful: *"Visit ok."*
6. **Next follow-up:** tap **In 2 days**, **In a week**, **In 2 weeks** or **In a month**, or pick a date and time.
7. **Save activity.**

That's it. BrewCRM then:

- puts the follow-up on your **Follow-ups** list and on the deal card;
- updates the account's *last activity*, so it won't show as going quiet;
- moves the deal forward when it's obvious: the first contact makes a Prospect **Contacted**, and a tasting makes it **Sampling**.

## 5. What BrewCRM does on its own

| Automatically… | How often | What you'll see |
|---|---|---|
| **Logs emails** between the connected Gmail mailbox and your contacts' email addresses | Every 15 minutes | *gmail* entries on the account's timeline |
| **Imports orders** from Loyverse for linked accounts | Every hour | *Order* entries with the items, plus the order count on the account |
| **Moves deals** to **First order** after the first purchase and to **Recurring** after the second | With each import | Cards move on the Pipeline |
| **Marks deals Dormant** when a customer hasn't ordered in 45 days | Every morning | The card moves to **Dormant**, a cue to visit |
| **Moves Dormant back to Recurring** when they order again | With each import | — |

Emails are only logged when the contact's **email address** is saved on the account, and only for the mailbox connected in Settings. Usually that's one shared brewery mailbox, so emails from your personal address won't appear unless your manager connects it. You can always log an email by hand.

## 6. A week with BrewCRM: Andrés in the West Valley

*Andrés sells for a small craft brewery. His territory is Escazú, Santa Ana and Ciudad Colón. He has about 40 accounts, 12 of them buying customers, and wants three new taps this month.*

### Monday: plan the week (20 minutes, at a desk)

1. **Follow-ups.** Six items are due this week, two of them in red from last week.
   - He calls Hotel Montaña Azul right from the list (**Call**) to confirm Wednesday's tasting.
   - Then he logs it: *Call · "Confirmed tasting Wed 3 pm with chef Luis"*, follow-up **In 2 days**.
2. **Going quiet.** Restaurante Don Pepe hasn't heard from anyone in 4 weeks.
   - He sends the owner a WhatsApp (the **WhatsApp** link on the account page): *"¿Cómo les va con la lager? Paso el jueves con la nueva edición de temporada."*
   - He logs it: *WhatsApp · "Offered to drop by Thursday with the seasonal"*, follow-up **In a week**.
3. **Pipeline.** He filters by `Santa Ana`:
   - three deals in **Sampling**, one in **Proposal**;
   - the Proposal card's follow-up date has passed, so it goes on his call list for today.
4. **Leads.** He searches *gastropub* in *Ciudad Colón* and gets 9 results.
   - He opens **Map** on each. Two look right, so he taps **Get phone**, then **Add as account**.
   - Three are chains, so he **Dismisses** them.
   - The two new accounts now sit in **Prospect** for Thursday's route.

### Tuesday: on the road (2–3 minutes after each stop)

He has four visits. **After each one, before driving to the next**, he logs it on his phone:

- **Bar La Esquina.**
  - *Visit · contact María · "Owner tried the IPA, wants pricing for 2 taps"*, follow-up **In 2 days**.
  - On the deal he sets **Stage → Proposal**, **Value per month** ₡150,000, **Est. liters** 60, **Next step** *Send price list*.
- **Pub El Faro (new).**
  - *Visit · "Manager out. Left samples + card with bartender Diego"*, follow-up **In a week**.
  - He adds Diego as a **contact**. BrewCRM moves the deal from Prospect to **Contacted**.
- **Café Central.**
  - *Visit · "Not interested: no tap system, bottles only from distributor"*.
  - On the deal he sets **Stage → Lost**, **Lost reason** *No taps; buys bottles via distributor*.
  - In the account's **Notes** he writes *"Revisit if they renovate the bar."*
- **Hotel Vista Verde.**
  - *Tasting · contact Chef Ana · "Liked the stout for the restaurant menu"*, follow-up **In 2 weeks**.
  - The deal moves to **Sampling** on its own.

That evening he emails María the price list from the brewery's Gmail. Fifteen minutes later it appears on Bar La Esquina's timeline, so he doesn't log it by hand.

### Wednesday: tasting day

- At the Montaña Azul tasting, he logs a **Tasting** with chef Luis: *"Wants the IPA + seasonal for the pool bar, 1 keg every 2 weeks"*.
- He moves the deal to **Proposal** with the value and liters.
- Back at the brewery he checks **Follow-ups**. Bar La Esquina is due Thursday, as planned.

### Thursday: closing and checking in

- María replies by email: *"Let's start with one keg."* It's on the timeline automatically.
- The first keg goes through Loyverse under the customer *Bar La Esquina*. He checks that the account is linked to that customer: **Loyverse → Link customer**, search *esquina*, **Link**.
- Within the hour the order appears on the timeline and the deal jumps to **First order** on its own.
- He drops by Don Pepe with the seasonal as promised and logs the **Visit**.

### Friday: review the week (15 minutes)

1. **Pipeline.** The *First order* column has one new card, *Sampling* has two more, and there's one *Lost* with a clear reason. The column totals show about ₡600,000/month of possible new business in Proposal.
2. **Follow-ups.** Next week is already filled with dates he set while logging, so nothing is forgotten over the weekend.
3. **Dormant.** One old customer, Licorera El Roble, slipped into **Dormant** this week. He sets a follow-up for Monday and adds the visit to next week's route.

### What his manager sees

Without asking Andrés for a report, the manager can see:

- what moved this week on the **Pipeline**;
- every conversation on each **account's timeline**;
- which customers are slipping, under **Going quiet** and **Dormant**.

If Andrés is sick, a colleague can pick up exactly where he left off.

## 7. Tips from the field

- **Log it before you start the car.** Two minutes now beats trying to remember five visits tonight.
- **Always set a follow-up.** An activity without a follow-up date is how customers get forgotten.
- **Write notes for someone else.** Names, preferences, opening days, who decides, what the competition offers.
- **One account per place, one deal per opportunity.** If a hotel has a pool bar and a restaurant buying separately, that's one account with two deals.
- **Add contacts' email addresses** so the brewery's emails with them are logged automatically.
- **Make sure orders go to a customer in Loyverse**, not as a walk-in sale. That's what moves deals to First order and Recurring on their own.
- **Mark lost deals honestly, with a reason.** It keeps the pipeline real and shows patterns ("no taps", "price", "exclusive with a big brand").
- **Fill in value and liters.** The column totals show whether you're on track. They also tell the brewer how much beer you're about to need.
- **Check *Going quiet* every Monday.** Keeping a customer is cheaper than finding a new one.

## 8. Questions

**I logged something on the wrong account.**
Open that account, find the entry on the timeline and tap **Delete**, then log it on the right one.

**A deal is in the wrong stage.**
Drag the card, or change the stage on the card or in **Edit**. Automatic moves only go forward, so a manual change sticks.

**I don't see the emails I sent.**
Is the contact's email saved on the account, and did you send from the mailbox connected in Settings? If not, log the email by hand.

**The Loyverse order didn't show up.**
Check that the sale was made to a customer (not a walk-in) and that the account is **linked** to that same customer. Orders import every hour; **Settings → Sync now** does it immediately.

**"Search Google Maps" is greyed out or says it needs setup.**
The Google Maps key isn't configured, or the monthly allowance is used up. Tell your manager.

**Can I use it on my phone?**
Yes, every screen works on a phone. On the Pipeline, change the stage with the menu on the card, or press and hold a card to drag it.

**Who can see my accounts?**
Everyone on the sales team sees all accounts. That way anyone can cover for anyone.
